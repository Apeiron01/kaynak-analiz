Curriculum and exercises

1. Shop plan

Define product type, target buyer, production capacity and a shop launch checklist.

2. Listing preparation

Prepare an example title, description, images and relevant attributes. Work from real buyer questions.

3. Pricing and operations

Build a cost worksheet and define shipping, fulfilment and customer communication steps.

4. Visibility and evaluation

Review search intent, listing consistency and available shop statistics. Separate views, enquiries and orders.

Before you begin

Who is it for?

Sellers preparing a new shop or improving an existing one. Bring a sample product, cost information and your main questions.

What will you leave with?

An example listing, cost worksheet and prioritised action list. Scope and session format are agreed before the programme.

Training or consulting?

Training builds your ability to run the workflow; consulting addresses implementation for your shop.

FAQ

Are sales guaranteed?

No. Exercises build a working process; sales depend on product demand, pricing, competition and execution.

Can I join without a shop?

Yes. A product idea or sample makes the exercises more useful. Confirm the programme scope before enrolling.

How are platform fees handled?

Use current fees for your country and shop. The worksheet is based on the official fee policy rather than fixed assumptions.
