CarQR: connected mobile and web workflows

CarQR connects a public QR scan flow with vehicle-owner alerts. A passer-by can send an alert from a browser without installing the app. The implementation includes Next.js, native Android/Kotlin and Firebase, with Android and iOS store listings. This demonstrates connected product workflows rather than a generic app mockup.
